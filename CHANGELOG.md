# Changelog

## [0.6.0](https://github.com/amariscalc/WinampNX2/releases/tag/v0.6.0)

- Search the SD card's `/music` folder and subfolders by filename, title, artist or album.
- Play search results or add individual tracks to the playback queue or favorites.
- Use the search screen with a controller or the touchscreen.

## 0.5.0

- Queue tracks to play next, including in shuffle mode, or append them to the playback queue.
- Save favorites to the SD card and browse them from MIX.
- Apply Flat, Rock or Hip-Hop EQ presets, or save and load a custom preset.
- View track details and embedded JPEG artwork.
- Switch between the spectrum analyzer and oscilloscope in the full-screen visualizer.

## [0.4.0](https://github.com/amariscalc/WinampNX2/releases/tag/v0.4.0)

- Rename saved M3U8 mixes and reorder or remove their tracks on the console.
